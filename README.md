# 🚀 Hackathon Team Management System

## 📌 About the Project

The Hackathon Team Management System is a web-based application
designed to help students and developers manage their hackathon
teams efficiently.

It helps team members organize team information, coordinate
activities, and manage their hackathon participation in one place.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

During hackathons, participants often face difficulties in
managing team members, organizing team information, and
coordinating project activities.

Managing these tasks manually can be time-consuming and confusing.

## 💡 Our Solution

Our Hackathon Team Management System provides a centralized
platform to simplify team management.

It aims to make collaboration easier, improve organization,
and help participants focus on building innovative projects.

## ✨ Features

- 👥 Team management
- 📝 Team member information
- 📊 Organized team details
- 🤝 Improved team collaboration
- ⚡ Simple and user-friendly interface
